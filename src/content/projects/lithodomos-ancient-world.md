## Overview

The Ancient World app with Lithodomos sits in a part of software I find especially interesting: domain-rich work where visual quality matters, but clarity still has to win.

## Problem

Educational and archaeological software can get messy fast. It needs enough richness to feel alive, but not so much that the interface collapses under its own ambition.

## My role

My contribution sits in the layer between product and engineering. That means helping make the front end more robust, translating specialist goals into workable software decisions, and keeping the experience understandable as the product grows.

## What makes it interesting

This is not generic CRUD work. It involves visualisation, pacing, performance, and deciding how much interactivity is actually useful. Those constraints make the product work better, not worse.
